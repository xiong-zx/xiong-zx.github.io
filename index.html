Research

Stochastic nonconvex optimization

I study optimization algorithms in the presence of randomness, motivated by the methods used in machine learning. I am interested in how noise shapes their behavior, what can be guaranteed, and how theoretical insights can inform algorithm design.

Learning while optimizing

I am also interested in decision-dependent optimization, where decisions influence the data available for learning. With a limited budget, how should we balance improving a decision with collecting new data? This perspective brings learning and optimization into the same iterative process.
